Privacy policy for the modern versions of Fourier, oScope, Octave, and Soundscape. Updated 2026-10-04.

Instruments & support

Microphone access

The apps use microphone input to draw live audio waveforms, spectra, octave bands, and spectrograms. Audio processing happens on your device. The apps do not upload microphone audio or analysis results to the developer or an external server. Capture stops when an app moves to the background.

Local data

The apps retain a short audio-analysis history in memory and save your display preferences locally. They do not continuously save audio recordings. When you request a data export, the app creates a local CSV file. You decide whether to save or share that file using the iOS share sheet and which destination to use.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The apps do not require an account and contain no advertising, tracking SDK, or developer-operated analytics service. They do not collect personal data for the developer. Apple handles App Store purchases and may provide its own platform diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Support and this website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and is subject to GitHub’s privacy policy. If you open a support issue, the information you choose to submit is visible on GitHub and is used to respond to your request. Support issues are public.

Your control

You can revoke microphone access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Removing an app removes its app-local preferences and files. Copies you export or share are controlled by the destination you select.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Alex Wiltschko through the support repository.
